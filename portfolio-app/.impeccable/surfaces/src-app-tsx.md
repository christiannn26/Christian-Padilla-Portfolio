# Surface: portfolio home (single page)

Scope: the whole one-page site (src/App.tsx and every section). Visitor mode: Experience, with Persuade duties (offer legible, contact always reachable).
Audience: service-business owners, GHL agencies and recruiters, weighted equally; often on a phone. Job: judge whether Christian can build their lead system. Action: explore the work, then contact (five channels) or download the CV.
Proof on hand: system flowchart, four live funnel demos, five workflow screenshots, YouTube walkthrough, certifications, education, experience. No testimonials, logos or metrics exist; none may be invented.
Constraints: copy and data locked (src/data and component copy); section order locked; keep dark + gold identity; mobile must stay easy to scan and tap; behaviors from the refactor (modals, keyboard access, lazy loading, reduced motion) preserved.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is Christian's own pipeline and the visitor is the lead moving through it: ten stages on a hairline rail, one gold lead token that advances as you read, and a journey that closes at Get In Touch. It refuses the dark-glass freelancer template of gradient headings, gold glows and interchangeable glass cards.

OWN-WORLD: Keynote-black stage (near-black grounds stepping up to raised graphite), ivory text, and a desaturated champagne gold reserved for "now": the active stage, the lead token, primary actions, live signal pulses. 1px hairline rules, black shadows only, no coloured glow, no gradient text. Bodoni Moda display set large and tight; Mona Sans for text, with wide tracked caps for stage labels and record fields read as label then value. Glass only on floating layers: nav, modals, HUD.

STORY: The visitor watches the system work (living flowchart, funnels, workflows, credentials), believes he builds the whole lead journey end to end, and reaches out or takes the CV.

Right: the portrait framed as a pipeline record card with a gold keyline and the "10+ Systems Built" field.
